# Curve and grading policy

The department applies a curve after raw scores are in. Rank the
class, then shift cutoffs so the median lands near a B-minus when
the raw median is far below that.

Do not curve individual quizzes. Only the course total is curved,
and only if the raw distribution is skewed low. A high raw median
stays as is. Students see both the raw total and the curved letter.

Write the rule on the syllabus so nobody is surprised at the end.
