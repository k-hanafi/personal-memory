# Teaching load committee

Alex sits on the department committee that sets load rules. Meetings
are once a month during term. [[dana-whitfield]] circulates the agenda
a week ahead.

The committee reviews release requests and decides what counts as a
full load. Minutes stay in the shared drive. Alex does not speak for
the committee outside those meetings.
