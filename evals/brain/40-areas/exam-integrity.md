# Exam integrity

Strategic exam sitting is the pattern to watch. Some students
take a deferred sitting so they land in a later, thinner group
when the curve is applied.

The curve itself is unchanged. Rank the class, then shift
cutoffs on the course total only, as in [[assessment-policy]].
Do not treat a deferred sitting as a reason to skip the curve
or to curve a single quiz.
