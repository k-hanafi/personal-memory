Small awards for empirical work
in labour and household
surveys. this is not a
teaching prize.

1. Purpose
The foundation will make a
limited number of awards in
the 2026 cycle. An award
covers research costs only.
Travel costs and data
costs may be listed. The
call does not fund teaching
release or summer salary.

2. Who may apply
Faculty at a university may
submit one application as
lead. A visiting scholar may
be named as a collaborator,
not as lead. Students may
not submit.

3. What the award may pay
Eligible costs:
  - survey fieldwork costs
  - data purchase costs
  - research assistant time
  - transcription costs
Ineligible costs:
  - building work
  - conference dinners
  - routine teaching
The size of an award is not
fixed in this call. Write the
costs you can defend. Do not
invent a ceiling the
foundation did not print.

4. Dates (cont.)
The
application deadline
is 2026-10-01. Staff
will not extend it by

5. What to send
One PDF. Aims of two pages.
A costs table of one page.
A short cv. No
appendices. The
application should name a
program officer only if the
foundation has already
assigned one. Cold names
are stripped.

6. Review
Internal screen, then two
readers. The foundation may
decline without a
reader report. An award
letter, if issued, will
restate the approved
costs line by line. Unspent
costs do not roll
forward.

7. Reporting
A one-page note at twelve
months. List costs
against the award, not
against a department
account. Do not send a
teaching dossier.
